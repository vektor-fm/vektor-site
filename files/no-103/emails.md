# The LEAKS emails

Five short emails for the two moments the film talks about: the first result, and the days before a trial ends. Replace everything in [SQUARE BRACKETS]. If a bracket asks for a number, use it only when the number is true for that user. If your app cannot measure it, pick the version without a number.

From the Vektor film "A thousand signups, two paying customers" (@vektor.fm).

## The first-result email

Send it once per user, the first time your app has clearly done its job for them, right away, from the place in your code where that happens.

### 1. With a number your app measures

```
Subject: [NUMBER] [THINGS] done in [APP NAME]

Hi [FIRST NAME],

You just [WHAT THEY DID, WITH THE REAL NUMBER: cleaned 2,140 rows / sent your first proposal in 4 minutes] with [APP NAME].

If you want to keep doing that, it is [PRICE] a month: [CHECKOUT LINK]

Your trial runs until [END DATE]. Everything you made stays in your account until then either way.

[YOUR NAME]
```

### 2. Without a number

```
Subject: Your first [THING] is done

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Your first [THING: proposal / report / clean list] is finished: [NAME OF THE THING THEY MADE].

That is what [APP NAME] does. If you want to keep it, it is [PRICE] a month: [CHECKOUT LINK]

Your trial runs until [END DATE].

[YOUR NAME]
```

### 3. The one from the film

```
Subject: You just [RESULT]

You just [RESULT: saved two hours / finished your first report], so keep it for [PRICE] a month: [CHECKOUT LINK]
```

"Saved two hours" and "$19" are the film's example. Put in your own true result and price.

## The trial reminder

Send it once per user, two days before the trial ends, only to people who are still on the trial.

### 4. Two days before

```
Subject: Your [APP NAME] trial ends on [WEEKDAY, DATE]

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Your free trial ends on [WEEKDAY, DATE].

If you subscribe ([PRICE] a month), nothing changes and you keep working as you do now: [CHECKOUT LINK]

If you do not, [WHAT REALLY HAPPENS: your account is locked but nothing is deleted / your projects become read-only].

[YOUR NAME]
```

### 5. Two days before, for someone who has used it

Only for users who have made something. The count has to be their real count.

```
Subject: [COUNT] [THINGS] so far, and your trial ends [WEEKDAY]

Hi [FIRST NAME],

You have made [COUNT] [THINGS] in [APP NAME] so far. Your free trial ends on [WEEKDAY, DATE].

To keep going it is [PRICE] a month: [CHECKOUT LINK]

If you do not subscribe, [WHAT REALLY HAPPENS TO THOSE THINGS].

[YOUR NAME]
```

## Three rules

- Say what really happens when the trial ends. If nothing is deleted, say so. Do not invent a loss to scare people.
- One link. The checkout, not the home page.
- If you do not know the number, do not write one.
