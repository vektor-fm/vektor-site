option A · recommended

The word

vektor

sprind's own nav is word-triggers — TATEN, WORTE, WIR — never a glyph. The whole system is typographic: weight 400, uppercase, hairline underlines. A word in Printvetica is the only option that belongs to it natively, and it toggles to CLOSE so the state is legible rather than inferred.

Start and Subscribe move into the drawer, which is where they belong on a phone — that is what clears the crowding. Biggest tap target of the three, and the only one that says what it does.

option B

Two long rules

vektor Start

Two rules rather than three, 24px rather than 15, drawn in #C9C9C9 — the site's exact hairline colour. It stops reading as an icon and starts reading as part of the rule system. Rotates into an X on open.

Keeps a glyph, so it stays language-neutral and compact. Quieter than A, and still needs one text link removed to breathe.
