VEKTOR /// PRIVACY POLICY / 10-2026

Privacy policy

This policy covers the Vektor website (vektor-fm.github.io/vektor-site), the Vektor newsletter, and the Vektor companion app used to operate our Instagram account (@vektor.fm).

Who is responsible

Vektor is built and run by Caelith Labs, Berlin, Germany. Caelith Labs is the controller for the data described here. Contact details are at the end of this page.

What the app does

The Vektor app responds to comments on our own Instagram posts. When you comment a keyword (e.g. "claude") on one of our posts, it sends you a single direct message containing the link we promised in that post, and may post one public reply under your comment.

Data we process

To do this, we process the comment you wrote, your Instagram username, and your Instagram user ID, as delivered to us by Meta's webhook. We use these solely to send the one reply you requested. We do not sell data, build profiles, run ads, or message you again unless you comment again.

Data we store

We store a short-lived technical marker of your comment's ID (not its content) for up to 30 days, only to make sure you never receive duplicate messages. Nothing else is retained.

Website

The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (GoatCounter) to count page views.

Newsletter: how you join

There are two ways onto the newsletter list. On this website, you enter your email address and we email you a link to confirm; you are only added once you click it. In an Instagram DM, after you comment on one of our posts, we may ask whether you want the newsletter; if you reply with your email address, that reply is your signup and you are added straight away, with no confirmation email. Either way you can unsubscribe at any time.

Newsletter: what we keep

For each address on the list we keep: your email address; where you signed up (the page on this site, or the Instagram post and DM); when you signed up, confirmed and unsubscribed; and your status (waiting for confirmation, subscribed, unsubscribed, or bounced). The wording you agreed to is the signup text shown at that place and time, and we keep every version of it in our version history. We use the list only to send you the newsletter. We do not sell it, share it for anyone else's marketing, or add you to any other list.

Newsletter: who processes it

Brevo (France) sends the emails: it receives your email address in order to deliver them, and keeps delivery and open/click statistics. Cloudflare runs our signup and Instagram endpoints (Cloudflare Workers) and stores the list in our own database (Cloudflare D1). The signup forms on this site use Cloudflare Turnstile to keep bots out: when a form loads, Turnstile checks your browser (it may read technical signals such as your IP address and browser details) and gives the form a token we verify when you sign up. We use its result only to accept or refuse the signup. Instagram (Meta) carries the DM conversation, including an email address you send us there, under its own terms.

Newsletter: how long we keep it

We keep your address for as long as you are subscribed. Every issue has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing stops all further emails at once. After you unsubscribe, or if your address bounces, we keep a suppressed record (your address, its status and the date) for one reason only: so we never email you again, including if your address is ever imported back by mistake. If you would rather we delete even that record, contact us (see below) and we will remove your address from our database and from Brevo.

Your choices & contact

To unsubscribe, use the link at the foot of any issue. To have your address or any stored marker removed, to get a copy of what we hold about you, or to ask anything about this policy, contact us at julianlaycock94@gmail.com or by DM to @vektor.fm. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.
