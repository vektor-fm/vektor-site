The newsletter and how you join

The Vektor newsletter covers AI tools, practical workflows, resource packs and explanations of how systems work. It may include occasional Caelith Labs notes about freelance work, examples and relevant service offers. It is the same newsletter, not a separate mailing list.

The website signup flow is designed to send a confirmation email. Follow its link and press the confirmation button to complete your subscription. Opening the link alone does not confirm it. The deployed confirmation settings still need verification before this draft is published. You do not have to join the newsletter simply to browse the website.

In an Instagram conversation, the newsletter invitation asks you to reply with your email address if you want to subscribe. This route currently uses single opt-in: the address can be added without an email confirmation. Replying with an address does not prove ownership of that inbox. The invitation also offers “skip” so that you can receive the resource link without joining. Commenting a resource keyword alone does not subscribe you. An address already marked as unsubscribed or bounced is not reactivated through this DM route.

Optional audience question, planned

An optional question, “What do you do?”, is planned for signup. It is not collected by the current signup form. If introduced, you will be able to skip it and still subscribe. The proposed use is to understand readers and choose useful topics. If an answer is linked to your email address, it is personal data, not an anonymous survey. The form will explain that use and ask for any consent it requires. It will not give permission for additional marketing.

Subscription and delivery records

The newsletter database holds your email address and a normalised version used to recognise duplicate inboxes. It also holds your status, signup source, signup and confirmation dates, unsubscribe date where applicable, and record creation and update dates.

Confirmation records include a random confirmation identifier, delivery attempts, the accepted-send time, any next retry time and error information. For new DM consents recorded through the updated flow, the record also holds the invitation wording, campaign and experiment variant. Older records may not contain that wording. Website records currently identify the signup source but do not store the exact wording in the subscriber row.

A delivery record can link your address to an issue, its send status, a provider message identifier, send-run identifier, timestamps and any error. A successful delivery record is not proof that you opened or read the email.

Recipient-level email open and click tracking has not been verified for this draft. Its settings, legal basis and disclosure must be settled before publication.

I do not sell the subscriber list or provide it for another sender's marketing.

Instagram messages and resource delivery

The resource flow processes the message or comment supplied through Instagram, identifiers needed to reply, and the progress of the request. It can include a follow check, a newsletter invitation, the resource link, a public reply and follow-up messages. Some messages that need a human answer are copied into an inbox record, including message text, sender and message identifiers, timestamp and a handling reason.

The flow also records which invitation variant was shown so that its results can be compared. This is not an anonymous record: it can be linked to an Instagram identifier. Meta also handles the conversation within Instagram. An email address sent in a DM passes through that service before it reaches the newsletter database.

Cookies, browser storage and analytics

GoatCounter says visitor counting does not use cookies or browser storage. It does process technical information: its policy says site, IP address and browser header information can be held in memory for up to eight hours to recognise repeat visits. That is different from storing an IP address in an analytics database. Its optional individual-pageview setting means “all analytics are anonymous” is not a verified statement for this account.

The Vektor site itself uses local storage to remember popup dismissals and, on some resource pages, which signup experiment variant a browser saw. This storage is separate from GoatCounter. The site also counts events such as form submissions, popup use and some outgoing clicks. Clearing site data removes locally stored values; blocking analytics requests prevents those requests from reaching the analytics service.

The Turnstile check

Website signup forms use Cloudflare Turnstile. Cloudflare describes signals including IP address, browser headers, TLS connection characteristics, and the site and origin. The signup server sends the challenge response and available IP address to Cloudflare for validation. A failed check prevents signup processing through that form.

Cloudflare says it acts as a processor when protecting this website and as a controller when using signals to improve its bot detection. The proposed basis for my security use is Article 6(1)(f).

Source: Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum, updated 18 June 2025, checked 1 November 2026. Turnstile is a security check, not consent to receive the newsletter.

Unsubscribing and withdrawing consent

Use the unsubscribe link in any issue, follow any confirmation step shown, or contact me by email, post or Instagram DM. Withdrawal does not require a reason and does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Newsletter consent is separate from consent to any optional tracking.

After your withdrawal is processed, your address must be excluded from further newsletter sends. A message already handed to the delivery provider may still arrive.

An erasure request is different from an unsubscribe request. I will delete data that is no longer needed, and explain any limited record that must lawfully remain, its purpose and retention period. I will not treat a request to delete data as permission to send again.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access to your data, correction, erasure or restriction of processing. Where the legal conditions apply, you can receive data you supplied in a portable format. You can withdraw consent at any time.

You can object at any time to use of your personal data for direct marketing, including related profiling. That use must then stop. You can also object, for reasons relating to your situation, to processing based on legitimate interests.

Send a request to julian@caelithlabs.com or the postal address above. If relevant, identify the email address or Instagram account involved so that the records can be found. Only information reasonably needed to verify the request should be requested. Rights requests are normally answered within one month; where the law permits an extension, the reason and extension must be explained within that first month.

You can complain to a data protection authority, especially in the EU country where you live or work or where you believe an infringement occurred. You do not have to contact me first. These rights follow GDPR Articles 12 and 15 to 22 and the complaint right in Article 77.

Providing an email address is voluntary, but it is needed to send you the newsletter. The reviewed flows use automated bot checks and experiment assignments; they do not show a decision producing legal or similarly significant effects under Article 22.
