VEKTOR /// PRIVACY POLICY / 07-2026

Privacy policy

This policy covers the Vektor website (vektor-fm.github.io/vektor-site) and the Vektor companion app used to operate our Instagram account (@vektor.fm).

What the app does

The Vektor app responds to comments on our own Instagram posts. When you comment a keyword (e.g. "claude") on one of our posts, it sends you a single direct message containing the link we promised in that post, and may post one public reply under your comment.

Data we process

To do this, we process the comment you wrote, your Instagram username, and your Instagram user ID, as delivered to us by Meta's webhook. We use these solely to send the one reply you requested. We do not sell data, build profiles, run ads, or message you again unless you comment again.

Data we store

We store a short-lived technical marker of your comment's ID (not its content) for up to 30 days, only to make sure you never receive duplicate messages. Nothing else is retained.

Website

The website is a static site. We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (GoatCounter) to count page views. If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email is used only to send it and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Your choices & contact

To have any stored marker removed or to ask anything about this policy, contact us at julianlaycock94@gmail.com or by DM to @vektor.fm.
