Claude Code · the session review

Claude Code that improves
itself from its sessions.

Claude Code writes every session you run to a folder on your computer and, by default, deletes each one after 30 days. That folder is a record of what you keep correcting and what you keep doing by hand. Below: the free plugin from the film, the three setup steps, and exactly what it does and does not do with your sessions.

Fig. 01 ·

Three steps.

What the DM promised. You need Claude Code, your own Claude plan, and Node.js 20 or later on your PATH (node --version). No npm install.

vektor-improve-plugin-v1.1.zip · 60 KB · MIT 1 · 2 · 3

Run it on your own sessions

setup.txt
1. Unzip vektor-improve-plugin-v1.1.zip

2. In a terminal, in the project folder you want the report in:
   claude --plugin-dir <unzipped>/vektor-skills/plugins/session-review

3. In Claude Code, type:
   /vektor:improve

It reads your recent local Claude Code sessions, writes a short report (what keeps going wrong, skills you are missing, lines to add to CLAUDE.md) and stages up to three skill drafts. Everything lands in .improve-output/ in the folder you ran it from. Keep that folder out of commits and cloud-sync folders.

Fig. 02 ·

What it reads, and what it doesn't do.

Read this before you run it.

1Your own sessions, on your own machine. The helper reads the session files under ~/.claude/projects and keeps only your own messages. It makes no network calls and has no analytics.
2Not fully offline. The masked evidence goes into your normal Claude Code conversation, so the analysis runs on your Claude plan and your plan's usage limits apply. No Vektor account, key or credits.
3Nothing installed, nothing edited. Drafts are review files. It never installs a skill, never edits CLAUDE.md or your settings, and never deletes your history.
4Review before you share. Secret-shaped strings are masked, but masking cannot catch everything, and project names and personal details in ordinary text stay in. Read the report and drafts before you share or record them.

Fig. 04 ·

Keep more than 30 days.

Optional. Claude Code deletes sessions older than cleanupPeriodDays when it starts; the documented default is 30. To keep more future history, merge this into your existing ~/.claude/settings.json (don't replace the file). Deleted sessions cannot be recovered.

~/.claude/settings.json
{
  "cleanupPeriodDays": 90
}
