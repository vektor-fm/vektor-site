Can you get it?

Check this before anything else. From OpenAI's “Meet dots”, section Access, read 30 September 2026. It is rolling out gradually, so an eligible plan may not show it yet.

Pro

Users over 18 outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. On Pro in the EU, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Switzerland or the UK, you will not see it for now.

Business Premium

Rolling out worldwide, Europe included.

Enterprise

Rolling out worldwide, off by default: a workspace administrator must enable it. OpenAI's launch post calls this route a beta.

What changed from agent mode.

Agent mode ran a task and stopped when the task did. A dot holds on to a goal between conversations.

It owns a goal between chats

Your dot runs on GPT-6 Astra with its own computer and browser in the cloud, so work can continue while your devices are off. It tracks progress, follows up as things change, and comes back with results or a decision that needs you.

The four rules.

In OpenAI's wording, with OpenAI's own one-line meaning. Set them in Settings → Personalization → Custom rules (under Permissions): Add, describe the action, pick one, Add rule.

1Take action without asking. Take the specified action without asking for approval.
2Take action when you say so. Proceed when you explicitly request the action; otherwise ask immediately before acting. (OpenAI's Help Center calls this tier “Take action if pre-approved”.)
3Ask before taking action. Ask for approval before taking the specified action.
4Hand off to you. Ask you to take the action instead.

What always stays with you

No rule switches these off: changing a password and transferring money. Your dot hands them over; open its computer, select Take over, then Return control when you're done.

Docs: learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/controls · help.openai.com, “Dots privacy, security, and safety FAQs” (read 2026-09-30)

Rules are instructions, not guarantees

OpenAI's words: custom rules are “instructions your dot tries to follow, and it can make mistakes”. Asking it to draft replies does not give it permission to send them. Start with clear instructions; add a rule only for a boundary that has to hold every time.

Fig. 04 ·

Set it up.

From OpenAI's “Get started with your dot”, read 30 September 2026. Desktop only to start: the ChatGPT desktop app or a desktop browser. Mobile web is not supported.

1Open dots in ChatGPT and follow the introduction.
2Connect apps such as email, calendar and files, or skip and add them later.
3In the desktop app, choose whether to connect your computer (it must stay online with the ChatGPT app open).
4Name it. The name updates its handle; the look is yours to change any time.
5Set its rules (Fig. 03), then give it the job you dread, close the chat, and check tomorrow.
