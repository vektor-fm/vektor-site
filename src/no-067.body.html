Install in Claude.

Surface labels can differ during the combined chat/Cowork rollout.

1Open Claude. Select Cowork first if your account still shows it as a separate tab.
2Choose the role plugin. Go to Customize → Plugins → Discover, then select Add.
3Authorize narrowly. Authenticate relevant services and grant only the files, connectors and actions needed.
4Test a bounded task. Use data you can inspect and review the result before relying on it or sending it.

Fig. 03 ·

Know what you're adding.

The source files are free and open source; product access, permissions and professional review remain separate.

Free source files; separate access

The official repository is Apache-2.0 licensed. Installing its files does not pay for Claude usage or grant an external service subscription. Cowork access and organization policy still apply.

Connected context needs actual access

A plugin supplies instructions and connector configuration. Credentials, permissions, complete data and current information still depend on your setup. The workflow states in the film are illustrations, not a captured connected Claude session.

Finance remains assisted work

Finance outputs need qualified professional review before reporting, filing or audit use. No financial result is claimed by this page or the film.
