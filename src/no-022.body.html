Step 01 / 04 · pick your install install

One command

The skills CLI drops it into any agent, global or per-project. Claude Code users can take the plugin route instead.

npx skills add blader/humanizer --global
/plugin marketplace add blader/humanizer
/plugin install humanizer@humanizer
Step 02 / 04 · run it run

Point it at anything

Paste text under the slash command, or just name a file — it rewrites in place and keeps every fact intact.

/humanizer [paste your text]
Humanize the prose in docs/launch-post.md
Step 03 / 04 · the two passes passes

Why it catches what you miss

Pass 1 rewrites against all 33 named patterns — "it's not this, it's that", the em dash, vague experts, the fake rule of three. Pass 2 is a cold audit: "what still reads obviously AI?" — then a final sweep fixes what the audit found. The catalog comes from Wikipedia's Signs of AI writing guide, written by the editors cleaning up the encyclopedia.

Step 04 / 04 · match your voice voice

Calibrate it to you

Feed it two or three paragraphs of your own writing first and it rewrites toward your voice, not a generic one.

/humanizer + "here's a sample of my writing…" + the AI text
