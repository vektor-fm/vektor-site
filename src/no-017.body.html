The match-
predictor prompt.

On July 14 we asked 9 AIs to pick the World Cup winner — zero said Spain to win. By July 17, six of seven had flipped. A chatbot asked "who wins?" retrieves narratives. Asked correctly, it calculates. This prompt is the "asked correctly" part — paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and get real probabilities instead of vibes. Free, no gate. As promised.

match-predictor.txt · paste into any AI
You are a football match forecaster. Use ONLY the data I paste below —
no outside knowledge, no narratives about form, momentum, or big names.

Data: [paste: last 10 results for each team, goals for/against,
xG for/against if you have it, home/neutral venue, key absences]

Output exactly:
(1) P(TeamA win), P(draw), P(TeamB win) as percentages summing to 100.
(2) The three data points that moved your estimate most, one line each.
(3) Your single biggest uncertainty.

Do not hedge with "anything can happen". Do not mention star players
unless they appear in the data.

Step 1 · the prompt ask

Force it off the story.

The prompt above does two things a normal question doesn't: it bans outside knowledge — no form narratives, no "big-game pedigree" — and it fixes the output format, three probabilities that must sum to 100, plus the data points that moved them. The model can't tell a story; it has to weigh what you pasted. Copy it once, keep it, reuse it every matchday.

data in → P(win) / P(draw) / P(win) · sums to 100 · no hedging
Step 2 · the data — all free feed

Where to get the numbers.

Three sources, all free, two minutes total. FBref team pages for the last 10 results and goals for/against. Understat for xG for/against — expected goals, the quality of chances behind the scoreline. The club's official X account for confirmed lineups and key absences. Paste all of it into the [data] slot.

FBref → results/goals · Understat → xG · club X → lineups/absences
Step 3 · the market sanity-check check

Check it against the money.

Convert the bookmaker or Polymarket odds to implied probabilities: 1 ÷ decimal odds for each outcome, then scale the three so they sum to 100% — that strips the bookmaker's margin (the vig). If your AI's number differs from the market by more than 10 points, trust the market unless you know something it doesn't — billions of dollars usually beat a chatbot's vibes.

P = 1 / decimal odds → normalize the 3 to 100% · gap > 10 pts → trust the market

The footnote · storyteller vs calculator why

They stop storytelling and start counting.

Asked "who wins tonight?", a chatbot follows the story — that's how our 9 AIs flipped France → Argentina → Spain in five days, tracking the headlines, not the numbers. Forced onto pasted data with a fixed output format, the same models stop retrieving narratives and start weighing evidence. Same AI. The question is the difference.
