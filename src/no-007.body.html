Not AI-from-scratch.

Nobody wrote 1.6M lines with AI. GeneralsX already had the engine on macOS/Linux, built on EA's GPL-v3 source. The part built with Claude is the iOS/iPad layer — touch input for a mouse-and-keyboard RTS, the mobile build, and the whole Metal render chain. Fork-then-port.
