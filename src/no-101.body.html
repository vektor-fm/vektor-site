The CLONE page · one repo, one template

Refill a reel that already works.

hypit is a GitHub repo that lets your AI agent, Claude Code or Codex, turn a reel that is already performing into a template: its footage, captions, B-roll and effects, all anchored to words instead of seconds. You refill it with your own script, voice and assets. Here is the setup from the video: the install line, the prompts in order, and what it costs.

Install once.

The install line

npx skills add hypit-ai/hypit -g

Run it in a terminal, or ask Claude Code to run it. It installs hypit as a Skill. On first use your agent checks for the Hypit executable and helps prepare it if needed, and your video project can live in any folder.

What you need

Node.js 22.15 or newer, and a coding agent: Claude Code, Codex or another agent that runs Skills.

Fig. 02 ·

The prompts, in the video's order.

Say them in your own words; these are the lines the film's terminal shows.

1 · Hand it a reel that is performing

> Turn this reel into a 1:1 template: <link to the reel>

Pick a reel that is already working in your niche. The agent downloads it and clones it as a workflow you can edit and re-run, not a one-off render.

2 · Your script and your audio

Write your own script, then clone your voice or record yourself reading it, and give the agent the audio file.

> Use voiceover.wav as the audio track

3 · Your assets, and how it should go

Put your photos, clips, and logos in the project folder, then use voice to text to tell the agent how the video should go, beat by beat.

> Use all of these assets

4 · Make the video

> Make my video

What comes back is the source reel's format with your voice and your assets in it. Rewrite a line and the timing follows the words.

What it costs.

The repo

hypit is free to use. Your coding agent and any model services have their own accounts and charges. Generation models are optional: a workflow can compile captions, motion graphics and code-rendered visuals into a finished video, rendered on your own machine, without calling a generation model. The README's two worked clones that did use generation models came to $1.15 and $1.07 in total.

The licence

A modified Apache 2.0. You can run it yourself for your own work, including commercial work and work for clients. A commercial licence is needed to run it as a hosted service for other people or to sell it. The videos you make belong to you.
