The footnote · how we grade how

Logged before. Graded after. All of it.

Every call is logged publicly before the event, where we can't edit it. When the event resolves, it's graded with the Brier score — the gap between the probability we gave and what actually happened, squared. Lower is better, and it rewards honest probabilities, not lucky picks: a confident wrong call costs more than a humble one. And we publish wins and losses — because a record you can't lose isn't a record.
