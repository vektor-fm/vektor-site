1   The hairline

You asked why the white rectangles aren’t visible. Two independent causes — and fixing only the colour would not have worked.

What we shippedrgba(255,255,255,.22)
sprind, both themes#c9c9c9  ← your choice
Full white#ffffff

Against black these read at roughly 56, 201 and 255 out of 255. sprind’s dark class swaps --white and --black but deliberately leaves --color-silver-300 alone, so #c9c9c9 is literally what their own dark bands use.

Cause two: closed rectangles barely exist in our markup

The technique that actually draws a rectangle is used only in our footer. Everywhere else we draw single borders, so at any colour most of the page would draw L-shapes. See section 2.

2   Three lattice techniques

They are not interchangeable. sprind uses all three, in different places.

Technique A · section dividers

border-bottom per module

Every section carries its own 1px bottom border. Section-to-section gap measures 0.00px — they abut. Our .stack > * { border-top } is equivalent.

Technique C · the true lattice

gap:1px + outline on every cell

Outlines paint outside the border box and take no layout space, so two neighbours’ outlines meet inside the 1px gutter and read as one continuous line. Footer and nav only.

5   The nav is invisible by default

This is the largest single gap. At scroll 0 the whole header sits off-screen at translate3d(0,-100%,0). It is revealed by hovering a 24px-tall invisible strip pinned to the top of the viewport — the green band below. Scrolling never reveals it. Once shown, it stays.

Bar 44px, 1px grid gap, submenu row 36.8px. Open adds panels at max-height 0 → 24rem over 0.5s ease-out, plus a full-width bottom row. Reveal is transform 0.4s ease. Desktop does not scroll-lock; mobile does.

8   Decisions

  1. ✓ Hairline — #c9c9c9, sprind’s own dark-theme value.
  2. ? Accent — sprind has none. Gold and red are defined in their tokens and used zero times; every hover is achromatic. Keep acid #39FF35 as a Vektor addition, or go fully achromatic and use their gap-widening + grey-to-white pattern?
  3. ✓ Banding — keep it, inverted.
  4. ✓ Hero — drop the image, go black + wordmark at their scale.
  5. ? Section inventory — match their section types (adding their stacked-card slider, podcast band, big-card modules, and rebuilding ARCHIVE as their 4-column grid with a 2×2 feature), or keep our IA and copy only the shared components 1:1?
