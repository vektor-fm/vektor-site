Superpowers is 14 composable Markdown "skills" that trigger automatically inside your coding agent and force a workflow: no code until the problem is brainstormed, planned, and pinned down by a failing test — and no "done" until the work survives review. honesty · what stars do and don't mean caveat

250,000 stars is popularity. Not proof.

The number is real — 263,036 stars puts obra/superpowers in GitHub's most-elite tier, alongside freeCodeCamp, awesome, build-your-own-x and coding-interview-university. But stars measure popularity, not code quality. Superpowers is a community framework that imposes process and discipline on your agent — it does not make an AI that never fails. What it changes is how failures surface: in a brainstorm, a plan, or a failing test, instead of in your repo.
